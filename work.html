Prior Work

Three projects that shaped how we run.

Small businesses, real budgets, real numbers. None of this was for a Fortune 500. All of it was for owners who had to feel the difference in their bank account.

Home Services

Northern Virginia plumbing outfit

They were spending around $2,500 a month on Google Ads and getting tire-kicker leads. We rebuilt the campaigns around emergency search intent, tightened the keyword set, and fixed the landing page. Same budget, roughly double the qualified calls inside 90 days. Owner told us his crew was booked three weeks out for the first time in two years.

Fitness Studio

Boutique studio losing members faster than it signed them

The ads were fine. The follow-up was broken. We built a new-member onboarding sequence, a review-ask flow, and a win-back series for lapsed members. Retention lifted about 2.8x over six months. Same staff, same classes. Just follow-up that actually followed up.

2.8xMember
retention

Landscaping

Fairfax landscaping company running ads in the dark

Around $1,800 a month in Google Ads with no tracking and no clue what it was producing. We rebuilt the account from scratch: proper conversion tracking, tight keywords, killed the broad-match leaks. Booked jobs per month went up about 90 percent. They actually cut spend after the rebuild and kept scaling from there.
